MY WORKFLOW

From Research to Educational Content

  1. Research: Review the topic, learning objectives and relevant supporting information.
  2. Content organization: Identify important concepts and develop a clear teaching sequence.
  3. Presentation design: Create slides, visual examples and supporting illustrations.
  4. Content review: Check clarity, accuracy and suitability for the audience.
  5. Video production support: Edit selected educational videos and refine their presentation.
