ABOUT ME

Research, data and meaningful impact

I am a research and data analyst with training in data science and a background in political science and sociology. My experience includes national household data collection, municipal statistics, customer-support analysis, web application development and geospatial research on access to primary healthcare in Ghana.

I am particularly interested in using survey data, GIS and statistical modelling to understand how location and social conditions affect access to essential services.

I also collaborate on educational content, developing research-based slides, reviewing concepts and editing videos that make complex subjects easier for students to understand.
